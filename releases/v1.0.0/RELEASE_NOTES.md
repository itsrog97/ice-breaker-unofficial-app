**Icebreaker (Unofficial) v1.0.0**: first public release of this product-management case study.

> Independent portfolio project, **not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Icebreaker Connect, Inc.** Users sign in with their own existing Icebreaker account.

### Try it
- **Live web demo (no install, no account, fictional data):** https://itsrog97.github.io/ice-breaker-unofficial-app/
- **Android:** download [`Icebreaker-Unofficial-v1.0.0.apk`](https://github.com/itsrog97/ice-breaker-unofficial-app/raw/main/releases/v1.0.0/Icebreaker-Unofficial-v1.0.0.apk). Android 7.0+, arm64 phones. Allow "install unknown apps" for your browser/Files app; it's signed with a test key, so Play Protect may warn: tap *Install anyway*.
- **Design review:** [`Icebreaker_Android_UIUX_Review_v1.0.0.pdf`](Icebreaker_Android_UIUX_Review_v1.0.0.pdf): 28 pages of wireframes, screens and functionality.

### What's in v1.0.0
Secure login (tokens in Android Keystore) · Home feed · Discover (search, 5 filters, sort, infinite scroll) · Person profile + Break the Ice · Chat (channels + direct messages) · Spark AI assistant with streaming replies · Notifications · My Profile · Settings (dark mode, change password) · tablet & Chromebook layouts · offline/error/empty states.

### Quality
45 automated tests · typecheck & lint clean · 16/16 end-to-end web-demo checks · earlier scripted QA against the live service on 5 screen sizes. See `CHANGELOG.md` and `docs/qa-report.md` for details and known issues.
